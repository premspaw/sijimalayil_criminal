High Court of Karnataka Division Bench Writ Appeal 2025
WA No. 1361/2023 (MV) • 2025:KHC:6104-DB

Bnd Energy Pvt Ltd vs State Of Karnataka
& Others

Appellant · Represented by Sri Siji Malayil, Advocate

Argued before the Division Bench led by the Hon'ble Chief Justice N.V. Anjaria regarding the implementation of High Security Registration Plates (HSRP) in Karnataka. The matter involved a challenge to the State Government's notification seeking to implement amended Rule 50 of the Motor Vehicle Rules.

The Division Bench, in its 2025 judgment, remitted the matter for expeditious final disposal while extending the interim protection that restrains the State from taking precipitative steps in the matter. This verdict is a critical milestone in the ongoing legal discourse surrounding license plate manufacturing and vendor approvals in the state.

High Court of Karnataka Civil Law Property & Marriage 2024
MFA (Civil) - Property Dispute

Mohamed Umar Seeni Ariff Khan & Ors
vs Mrs. Tanzia Bano

Respondent · Represented by Sri Siji Malayil, Advocate

The High Court of Karnataka dismissed multiple appeals challenging temporary injunctions in a civil suit for declaration, partition, and injunction. The dispute centered on marriage validity, property ownership, and possession rights, where the respondent demonstrated a prima facie case based on documentary and circumstantial evidence.

The court affirmed that marriage validity can be established even without registration under the Foreign Marriage Act, 1969, provided evidence like communication and financial records supports the claim. The interim injunction remains in effect until the final disposal of the suit.

High Court of Karnataka Constitutional Law FCRA Writ Petition 2023
WP No. 1234/2023 (GM-RES)

Manasa Centre for Development & Social Action
vs Development Credit Bank Ltd & Others

Petitioner · By Sri Siji Malayil, Advocate

A registered NGO holding permanent FCRA registration had its designated bank account frozen by Development Credit Bank on instructions from the Ministry of Home Affairs, which had placed foreign donor 'Dan Church Aid' in a Prior Reference Category. The petitioner successfully argued that its FCRA registration entitled it to receive funds from other donors held in the same account, which were unrelated to Dan Church Aid.

High Court of Karnataka Criminal Law Section 482 CrPC 2022
CRL.P No. 8842/2022

Quashing of False F.I.R in Cyber Crime Allegation

Petitioner · By Sri Siji Malayil, Advocate

Successfully secured the quashing of a criminal proceeding involving alleged digital fraud. The High Court accepted the contention that the continuation of proceedings in the absence of prima facie evidence would amount to an abuse of the process of law.
